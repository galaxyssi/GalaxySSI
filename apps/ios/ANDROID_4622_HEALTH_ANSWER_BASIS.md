## Scope

Add a collapsed Answer basis row beneath actual answer text in the existing
private Health Agent transcript. Ordinary chats, composer, header, pending rows,
plans, interrupted controls and imported legacy replies are unchanged. This is
not a general model-citation viewer or an independent medical verification.

At the existing validated begin-turn transaction, retain a small source projection
with the encrypted turn: exact current measurement and comparison references,
the approved recent history page, explicitly selected related measurements,
current-record confirmed context, actually included selected profile metadata,
guideline label and confirmed target. Capture only their metadata, not result
values, profile answers, excerpts, prompts, credentials or original filenames.
The grant binds owner, conversation, thread, turn, concern/revision, destination,
permission IDs, profile selection epoch, related scope and research authorization.
Unbound threads or legacy turns have no reconstructed grant. Separate visit-journal
and instruction discussions do not emit this concern-turn footer; their distinct
consent flows are not relabeled as a care grant.

Public-source metadata is added only from same-binding local native retrieval
receipts with allowed URLs, matching content-hash shape and ordered observation
times. Model prose and model-generated source titles/URLs are never inputs.
This projection lists the approved initial history page, not unrecorded older
tool pages or an inferred complete health archive. Profile/guideline timestamps
remain absent when the approved iOS payload did not supply them.

Reading or expanding rechecks current care/online permissions, approval lifetime,
concern/assessment validity, record revision, selected profile and related scope,
and standing research authorization where applicable. It does not reauthorize
requests or execute tools. Only a retained actual answer can display the footer.
Expansion locally resolves names only from the exact approved record revision,
metric, measurement time and captured original ID/hash. Current primary/secondary
evidence linkage must still match. Review-only, removed, missing or mismatched
originals cannot reveal a filename. Existing encrypted header verification and
restore leases are reused; file bodies are not loaded for labels. Recheck the
grant after original-index reconciliation before publishing resolved names.

The view clears mounted metadata and expansion on owner/session/privacy changes,
background/lock, record/profile/original-index changes, disappearance and approval
expiry. Expansion starts from a new check, never retained expanded plaintext.
Like the existing Health Records APIs, metadata access is MainActor-isolated;
this change does not introduce a new background database reader or polling loop.

Prompt guidance preserves a confirmed fasting label while acknowledging unknown
hours: ask duration only if it changes the next decision. No hours or missing
negative answers are inferred. No Android/Desktop, version or backend changes.

## Verification boundaries

Ten synthetic Swift test methods are authored for scoped capture, legacy absence,
unanswered/foreign-turn reads, expiry/revocation, changed measurements, approved
history bounds, exact report identity, missing/review-only originals, authenticated
retrieval metadata, encrypted reopen and fasting-label uncertainty. They have not
been compiled or executed. Synthetic inputs are not device or clinical evidence.

The focused JavaScript guard checks actual wiring, resource/project registration,
and exact preservation of existing record authority, native tools, originals and
ordinary-chat code. Source checks do not validate Swift compilation, UIKit
lifetimes, encrypted migrations, storage concurrency, cryptography or a device.

No native build/typecheck, XCTest, device/provider testing, medical acceptance,
CI monitoring, full repository check or IPA. No private data, raw reports or
device evidence uploaded. Remaining Android-to-iOS parity work is not complete.
