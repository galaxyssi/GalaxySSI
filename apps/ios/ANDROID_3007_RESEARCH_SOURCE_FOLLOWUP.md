# Research Source Membership Follow-up

Baseline: main 49c2522d05cef989f00fcde7474bb38d117bc7fb. Independent increment after foreground #4565, not a replay of open cognition #4566. This branch does not require #4566 to merge. No Android/Desktop production changes.

## Implemented

- Research tasks gain optional sourceConversationIds. Synthesized Codable preserves the existing camelCase schema and decodes absent optional proof as nil. No automatic deletion or fabricated legacy proof.
- Cognition-derived research preserves the primary and complete existing source_conversation_ids metadata. SESSION_PRIVATE and evidence-invalidated cognition cannot seed work. Long-horizon records require matching host goal metadata and a complete-source key before their synthetic internal identity can be omitted. Such identifiers are not passed to conversation-history lookup as actual conversation IDs.
- Current known non-private/non-paused sessions constrain new seeds, the outer task selection, the executor's own internal selection, initial backend dispatch, cloud success/failure publication and response consumption. The optional eligibility API defaults preserve other policy callers; the production research cycle supplies the same predicate to BOTH selectors.
- Research context requires an eligible task before history access, filters research items and supplies the live conversation allow/exclude sets to the synchronous realtime provider. Public-attention work keeps its existing empty private context and dedicated policy checks.
- Only a request exactly present in the saved dispatch list can be dispatched. Non-public resources must retain their current transport-specific permission and contact identity; paired contacts remain verified and non-deleted. Local research requests use the actual resource contact generated by targetContactId, not the empty contact convention used by cognition requests.
- Response acceptance requires a positive correlation, unique matching saved request, exact resource contact and matching task. Nonblank conversation/turn/task response fields must match; blank legacy fields remain compatible, not cryptographic ownership proof. Existing public-attention strict matching/captured-evidence checks remain in addition to these gates. Failed persistence cannot publish new proactive results from the consumer.
- Stale lease reconstruction preserves the complete optional source set instead of losing it on retry.

## Incomplete / Not Claimed

- Missing proof fails closed. Legacy non-public research, autonomous-created research and evalops knowledge-gap research do not gain inferred authorization merely from a primary ID or synthetic agent-evalops ID. Those producers still need authoritative complete-source integration; their unproven records remain stored but are not selected. This is an intentional restriction, NOT complete research feature parity or a migration/re-enqueue UI.
- The task field tracks declared source-event conversation membership, NOT every conversation that contributed to historical cognition prose, retrieved context, unit evidence or synthesis. Original selected-context provenance and its later revocation remain a separate gap. Do not interpret this as complete background privacy acceptance.
- Shared local-model callback publication, autonomous response listeners, existing outbox records, already sent prompts, running inference/network/tool effects and archived evidence are not retroactively cancelled, removed or comprehensively scoped by this change. Local research result consumption is gated; this increment does not add the cognition-only callback hook from #4566 to research.
- No atomic session/task transaction, cross-process/shared-file CAS, storage fault/restart acceptance, per-token disk checks, resource replacement or new OS background grant. Current source snapshots can change after a read; gates are boundary checks, not atomic revocation.
- Existing historical whole-file guards may need explicit composition with these new bounded hooks. The new guard does not claim that all earlier whole-file guards, the full npm check suite or all Android #3007 requirements pass.
- No native build/typecheck, XCTest execution, CI watching, device/model/network/privacy/performance acceptance or IPA. Overall #3007 and the full Android PR backlog remain incomplete.
