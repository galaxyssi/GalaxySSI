# Collaborative Evolution Acceptance Scope

This audit separates the eleven requested engineering increments from claims
about autonomous intelligence. The user authorized compilation, local synthetic
tests and S26U installation, but explicitly excluded real-model research runs.

## What Acceptance Means Here

Passing fixtures establishes executable integration, precise identity and scope,
evidence/measurement gates, rejection of known invalid cases, dispatch/recovery
bindings and encrypted persistence. It is not a real-model completion score.
An intentionally synthetic report is not evidence of an actual novel discovery.

No fixed research-stage or round count declares a user goal complete. Conversely,
permission boundaries, explicit user controls, resources and qualified acceptance
cannot be removed by an Agent's self-improvement proposal.

## Deferred Effectiveness Evaluation

Real-model validation remains deliberately outside this development acceptance:

- Does the Agent independently find the important bottleneck, rather than a fixture supplying it?
- Does its experiment actually measure the stated property and avoid leakage/confounders?
- Does innovation survive independent, held-out and cross-domain evaluation?
- Does a team outperform one Agent at the same total cost?
- Are predictions calibrated outside the synthetic environment?
- Do generated tools remain safe under adversarial inputs and permission boundaries?
- Does a long-running campaign improve while retaining old capabilities?
- Do prolonged offline, Doze and physical-reboot workloads preserve the complete new path?

These questions need separately authorized real-model, long-duration and
independent evaluations. No claim of scientific autonomy, general intelligence,
continual model-weight learning or superiority over another product is made.
