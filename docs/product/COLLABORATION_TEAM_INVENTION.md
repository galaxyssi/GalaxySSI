# Evidence-Bound Team Invention

## Purpose

Goal 6 turns peer interaction into a versioned, testable shared method. It extends the existing
workspace, innovation work binding and durable DAG. There is no additional model loop, idle
polling, broadcast fan-out, UI, execution authority or fixed number of research stages.

## Mechanism

1. Members explore different mechanisms and preserve independent source ideas.
2. A non-contributor records a `team_exchange` challenge with a specific issue, proposed change,
   uncertainty and discriminating test. An idea contributor records a linked response: adopt,
   adapt, reject or test-needed. Disagreement remains visible; a response is not verification.
3. `team_synthesis` identifies each source idea, challenge, response, retained mechanism, changed
   part and effect of the exchange. It describes the interaction hypothesis and why the result
   is not concatenation. At least two distinct source ideas from different members are needed
   to claim a team synthesis; that is a meaning constraint, not a research-step limit.
4. A combined `innovation` preserves the synthesis, every source idea and the original opportunity.
   All participants remain contributors, including critics. Renaming the combined method cannot
   turn a contributor into an independent reviewer.
5. Members preregister ordinary paired experiments against every source idea and a distinct
   single-author control. All plans must precede all campaign trials and differ only in baseline.
   Dataset revisions, cases, method, source, environment, resource scope and budget stay identical.
6. The existing executor runs authorized tools. Original reports include exact dataset hashes,
   measurements and preparation/coordination/execution resource totals for each variant. Each
   total must fit the same registered ceiling; execution totals cannot omit known trial costs.
   Retention also aggregates each method's costs across the complete campaign, so splitting
   comparisons cannot hide an overrun. Shared preparation is attributed once, not omitted.
7. An independent `team_evaluation` reads every original report, checks the harness, preserves
   uncertainty and either continues, revises, rejects or retains the scoped method. Retention
   requires positive target gains and passing regressions against every control. Missing costs,
   omitted parents, mismatched conditions and incomplete results cannot become team success.

These are optional, goal-driven activities, not a mandatory sequence. Agents can keep several
alternatives, revise a hypothesis, schedule an informative test, or use a simpler single-agent
method when it performs better. No count decides that the research objective is complete.

## Actual Execution And Recovery

`innovation_work.phase` accepts `challenge`, `respond` and `combine`, in addition to the existing
exploration, prototype, experiment and assessment phases. The same next-round and incremental
planners admit these tasks. They pin exact references, member identity and assignment under an
immutable work ID. A response is assigned to an idea contributor; a challenge to a non-contributor.
Combination work can precede the combined idea, but needs the preserved synthesis to build from.

The worker sees these references as task data through the common research prompt, including
managed cloud and remote Desktop members. Full schemas are fetched through existing paged recall.
Completed work outcomes remain durable and are not automatically replayed after reconnection.
Unrelated branches need not wait for a challenged idea. Pause, permissions and qualified original
goal acceptance remain unchanged.

Team-derived innovation assessments, retained lessons and procedure skills preserve the comparative
evaluation. Iterative lineage checks cover source ideas, exchanges, synthesis, datasets, single-agent
control, plans and results. Changed sources mark old records historical without deleting evidence.

## Interpretation Limits

- The host validates scope, identity, references, original-report provenance and arithmetic. It
  cannot certify the creativity or scientific validity of a mechanism described in prose.
- Different member IDs do not prove independent reasoning. Current-round blind-work isolation
  is retained; genuine model independence requires a separately controlled experiment.
- Cost totals are original tool-reported accounting, not independently audited billing. A faulty
  harness can lie. Resource scope, harness inspection and external replication remain necessary.
- Equal budget means the same preregistered end-to-end ceiling, not forced equal expenditure.
- A passed comparison establishes only the stated local scope. It does not establish general
  team superiority, global novelty, physical experimental completion or goal acceptance.
- Local fixtures validate the mechanism, not autonomous generation of the ideas in that fixture.
