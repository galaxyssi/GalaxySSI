# Goal-Bound Innovation And Verification

## Purpose

The fifth dedicated collaborative-evolution increment connects autonomous idea generation to
the existing durable work graph and original acceptance criteria. Members may identify missing
knowledge, contradictions, limitations or cross-domain opportunities, compare alternative routes,
prototype a mechanism, register a discriminating experiment, and revise from evidence. These are
agent-selected actions, not a mandatory six-step pipeline or a research-round limit.

No new UI, background model loop, retry counter, permission grant or provider is introduced.
Cloud, native and remote Desktop members receive the same compact policy and paged record rules.
Existing executors, checkpoints, pause/stop controls, scoped recall and delivery semantics remain
authoritative. Original physical/scientific acceptance requirements cannot be replaced by simulation.

## Durable Records

- `innovation_opportunity`: original goal hash, exact criterion ID/requirement, unmet need,
  expected benefit, constraints, null hypothesis, alternative routes, discriminating test and
  evidence-linked drivers. A knowledge gap needs a saved gap/diagnosis; cross-domain work needs
  a transfer study; contradictions name both claims. Registration remains a hypothesis.
- `innovation`: optionally binds an exact opportunity and preserves it as a parent, alongside
  existing mechanism, prediction, falsifier, risk and prior-art fields.
- `innovation_assessment`: independent comparison of closest observed prior work, scoped
  novelty judgment, actual feasibility, measured value, limitations, unresolved issues and next action.
  Partial, negative and inconclusive decisions are retained instead of erased or marked complete.

Opportunity and assessment records are immutable. Ideas can be revised by their author, with
exact ancestry. Mixed innovation/transfer ancestry is checked iteratively, not with a recursive
depth limit. Changed source, idea or dataset revisions require revalidation before new admission;
historical records remain readable.

## Actual Work, Not Metadata Alone

Ordinary DAG work can add `innovation_work` with an exact opportunity, agent-selected phase,
expected output and reason to act now. Prototype/experiment/assessment/revision work also names
an exact idea. Experiment execution requires its saved preregistered plan. Optional prior
assessment must belong to that same idea.

Both next-round and live graph admission check the real request's goal hash and preserved
criterion, pin exact versions into worker context, and persist an immutable claim per work ID.
No unrelated branch must wait. Replayed admission cannot change the member, phase, assignment,
goal or references; changed work gets a new ID. Invalid mixed batches dispatch nothing.
Ordinary work has a fast path with no innovation workspace lookup.

Terminal execution outcomes reach the existing coordinator with task identity and output digest.
The host explicitly marks them as *not* innovation verification or goal completion. The model
must inspect reports and decide a useful next question, alternate hypothesis, peer request or
resource condition. Work completion alone cannot retain a method.

## Three Separate Judgments

| Dimension | Required basis | Not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novelty | Closest original observations, overlap/difference/significance, search scope and gaps | Worldwide novelty or complete literature coverage |
| Feasibility | Preregistered absolute threshold on candidate measurements | Suitability outside the measured environment |
| Practical value | Positive target gain explicitly registered as `dimension=value`, preserved baseline and passing regressions | General usefulness or team superiority |

Feasibility cases use `purpose=feasibility` and a finite threshold. Candidate mean must meet or
exceed it for maximization, or stay below it for minimization. They retain existing source,
time, environment, budget, variant and repetition checks. A feasibility-only experiment yields
`feasibility_measured`, never retained capability. Incomplete or infeasible comparisons cannot
authorize retention.

An assessment can retain only a scoped distinction with measured feasibility and value, no
unresolved issues, and a reviewer who did not author the opportunity, idea, baseline, plan or
trial. Every original source/report must have been served in full to that reviewer. Opportunity-
driven lessons and reusable procedures require the successful exact assessment, closing the
otherwise possible generic-lesson bypass.

## Limits And Scientific Honesty

The host checks provenance, read coverage, bindings and arithmetic. It does not prove that a
harness is honest, that a metric is causally meaningful, that sources are exhaustive, or that a
model understood every document. Labels and novelty significance remain explicit member judgments.
Different member identities do not establish independent reasoning. Reviewer inspection, sound
study design, external replication and qualified validators remain necessary.

Local synthetic tests validate these contracts. They do not demonstrate autonomous scientific
innovation, real-model quality gains or superiority over single-agent research. No real-model
test or original protein-research run is authorized for this increment's installation test.

## Design References

The design adapts separation of generation, critique, comparison and evidence-driven evolution
from [Google's AI co-scientist](https://research.google/blog/accelerating-scientific-breakthroughs-with-an-ai-co-scientist/)
and the experiment/report loop in [The AI Scientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06292).
These are architectural references, not evidence that GalaxySSI reproduces their capabilities.
