# Android Body Exploration

Health Center's function menu opens the h24 education page. It uses the
concept illustration supplied with the Android V2 design, resized and
encoded for Android as a WebP resource. The illustration is not a scan, an
anatomical diagram, or a representation of the user. The full-size viewer
reuses the app's pinch and pan surface.

The native page supports organ, tissue, cell, and molecular explanations for
circulation, breathing, metabolism, nerves, digestion, and kidney function.
Directed diagram stages have their own text and labels; color alone does not
communicate direction or meaning. Circulation and breathing also have a
separate qualitative hemoglobin/oxygen lesson. The drawing represents a
process sequence, not a three-dimensional molecular structure or anatomical
simulation.

Playback advances one diagram at a time, with pause, slow motion and
previous/next controls. Leaving the lesson or backgrounding the app cancels
the animation. When Android disables animations, manual navigation remains
available.

The circulation experiment calculates a fictional example of cardiac output
from beats per minute and milliliters per beat. Its input ranges are
interaction limits, not reference ranges or medical recommendations. The
study surface does not read health records, start network requests or put
values into a chart. The separate personal measurement command opens the
existing blood pressure trend page only on user action.
